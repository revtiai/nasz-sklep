Your online shop on Shopify:
answers to your questions

For Fathey and the team. Short answers first, then the detail. Everything here can be set up for you before the shop goes live.

1Can stock update itself?

Yes. When something sells out, the website can show it as sold out on its own.

  • Shopify keeps a stock count for every product. When it reaches zero, the product shows as sold out online, and it comes back when you add stock.
  • If the shop's till is Shopify POS (Shopify's own till app), the shop and the website share one stock count, so a sale at the counter updates the website straight away.
  • If you use a different till or stock system, many of them (for example Epos Now) can connect to Shopify and keep stock in step. Tell us which system you have and we'll check it works.
  • Without a connection, staff update stock in the Shopify app. It takes seconds per product, and we'll show them the quickest way.

2How easy is it for the staff?

Most of the day is four taps on a phone or tablet.

1. New orderThe phone pings with the order and what to pack.
2. Pack itPick the items, tick them off.
3. ReadyTap “Ready for pickup”. The customer gets a message.
4. DoneMark it collected or delivered.
  • Prices and stock: open the product in the Shopify app, change it, save. A photo can be taken on the phone.
  • New products: we load everything from the spreadsheet in one go, so nobody types them in one by one.
  • We'll set it all up, give the team a short walkthrough and leave a one-page cheat sheet by the till.

3£15 delivery over 10 miles and a £40 minimum

Yes. Delivery by distance is built into Shopify. It's already working on the demo.

  • Shopify's local delivery works out the distance from the shop to the customer's address. We set free within 10 miles and £15 further out, up to the furthest distance you want to deliver.
  • A minimum order can be set for delivery in Shopify itself. To make £40 the minimum for every online order, including collection, we add a small add-on app.
  • Try it on the demo: add items, go to the basket, choose Home delivery and type a postcode.

4Only taking orders at certain times

Yes, with an add-on app. Good idea while the team learns the system.

  • Shopify doesn't do this on its own, so we add an app that sets your online ordering hours and the collection and delivery time slots customers can pick. It can also cap how many orders come in per slot.
  • You can widen the hours as the team gets more confident, or pause online orders on a busy day.
  • Try it on the demo: the Online orders screen has the hours and a pause button, and the website follows them.

5Can each member of staff log in?

Yes, depending on the Shopify plan.

  • Shopify's Basic plan has the owner's login only, with no extra staff logins. A simple way to run it is one tablet signed in at the counter for packing orders.
  • The Grow plan includes up to 5 staff logins, so each person has their own and can only see what they need.
