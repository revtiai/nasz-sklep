Your online shop on Shopify:
answers to your questions

For Fathey and the team. Short answers first, then the detail. Everything here can be set up for you before the shop goes live.

1Can stock update itself?

Yes. Your till, EPOS Direct, connects to Shopify.

  • EPOS Direct lists a Shopify connection that keeps stock in step between the till and the website. It also brings across products, prices and categories, and sends online orders to the till.
  • So when something sells out at the counter, it shows as sold out online, and it comes back when you add stock.
  • The till already has every product with its price and barcode, so it can fill most of the online shop for us. We then add photos and the English and Polish names.
  • Ask EPOS Direct to switch on the Shopify connection, and whether it costs extra on your package.

2How easy is it for the staff?

Most of the day is four taps on a phone or tablet.

1. New orderThe phone pings with the order and what to pack.
2. Pack itPick the items, tick them off.
3. ReadyTap “Ready for pickup”. The customer gets a message.
4. DoneMark it collected or delivered.
  • Prices and stock: open the product in the Shopify app, change it, save. A photo can be taken on the phone.
  • New products: we load everything from the till or the spreadsheet in one go, so nobody types them in one by one.
  • We'll set it all up, give the team a short walkthrough and leave a one-page cheat sheet by the till.

3Delivery and the £40 minimum

Set up exactly as you asked. It's working on the demo.

  • Shopify's local delivery works out the distance to the customer: free within 10 miles, £15 from 10 to 20 miles, and collection only beyond 20 miles.
  • Shopify can set a minimum for delivery. To make £40 the minimum for every online order, collection too, we add a small add-on app.

4Online orders from 10am to 6pm

Yes, with an add-on app. That's how the demo is set.

  • The app sets the ordering hours and the time slots customers pick. You can widen the hours later, or pause orders on a busy day.

5Can each member of staff log in?

Yes, depending on the Shopify plan.

  • Basic has the owner's login only: one tablet signed in at the counter works well. Grow includes up to 5 staff logins.

6Alcohol and vapes

  • Alcohol can be sold online with an age check: it's marked 18+ and staff check ID when it's collected or delivered. Check the shop's alcohol licence covers online and delivery sales.
  • Vapes are a restricted product for Shopify's card payments: they need special approval and are often refused. So the demo shows vapes as in the shop only. Selling them online would need a separate payment provider.
